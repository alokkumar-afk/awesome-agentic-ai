# Code of Conduct

Awesome Agentic AI is intended to be a useful, professional, and welcoming open-source resource.

By participating in issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, or other repository activity, contributors are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully.
- Critique technical ideas and claims rather than people.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Disclose relevant affiliations when recommending projects.
- Avoid spam, harassment, discrimination, threats, and abusive behavior.
- Respect maintainers' curation decisions even when you disagree.
- Avoid manipulating discussions, reactions, or pull requests for promotional purposes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples include:

- Personal attacks or harassment.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Repeated promotional posting after a resource has been declined.
- Concealing a commercial relationship while promoting a project.
- Coordinated spam or link-building campaigns.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately misleading maintainers or contributors.
- Attempting to introduce malicious links, scripts, workflows, or repository configuration.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove contributions that violate these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may result in restrictions from participating in the repository.

If a disagreement is technical, explain the evidence and reasoning. Good-faith disagreement is welcome.
